Observer — how to track anomalies

A camera, a small edge computer and self-learning AI that works out what “normal” looks like on its own — and raises an alarm the moment the scene stops matching it.

AI

YOLOv8 finds and tracks objects; their positions, speeds and relations become features — and a model learns “normal”.

Anomaly detection

Every frame is scored. Outliers raise an alarm with score and snapshot — live on the web dashboard.
